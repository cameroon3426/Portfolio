The Problem

A service team receiving high volumes of technical support emails had no consistent process for drafting replies. Responses were slow, knowledge was siloed, and senior engineers were constantly interrupted for questions the documentation already answered.

The Solution

An N8N cloud workflow monitors Gmail every 15 minutes, classifies incoming support emails, embeds questions via Gemini, searches a Pinecone vector knowledge base, and creates a Claude-drafted reply directly in Gmail Drafts. A human reviews every draft before sending — the system never auto-sends.

The Results

Eliminates blank-page syndrome for support replies. Draft quality validated at 85%+ accuracy against the knowledge base. Response time reduced from hours to minutes. Senior engineers freed from tier-1 support entirely.
