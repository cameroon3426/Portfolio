My QIROX is showing fault 1052 on startup. What does that mean and how do I fix it?
Fault 1052 is a drive enable failure. Here's how to resolve it:
1
Check the E-stop circuit — all segments must be in RUN position before drives can enable.
2
Navigate to DIAGNOSTICS > Drive Status and confirm each axis shows READY state.
3
If fault persists after reset, inspect I/O board connector X14 for loose contact or damage.
